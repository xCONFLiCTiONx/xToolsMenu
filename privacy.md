# Cookie-Manager Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

Cookie-Manager helps you maintain your privacy by automating cookie cleanup.

## 1. Data Handling
- **Whitelisting:** Your whitelisted domains are stored locally in your browser's synchronized storage (if enabled in your browser settings) or local storage.
- **Cleanup Logic:** The extension monitors tab closures to trigger the removal of site data. This process happens entirely within your browser.

## 2. No Telemetry
We do not collect statistics on the sites you visit or the cookies being managed.
